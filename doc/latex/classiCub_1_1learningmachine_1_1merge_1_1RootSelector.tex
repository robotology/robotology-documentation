\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
The \doxylink{classiCub_1_1learningmachine_1_1merge_1_1RootSelector}{Root\+Selector} is entry point for a format bottle. 

It inherits most of its functionality from the \doxylink{classiCub_1_1learningmachine_1_1merge_1_1CompositeSelector}{Composite\+Selector}, with the primary difference being that it does {\itshape not} wrap its contents in another Bottle.
