\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A class for computing joint force/moment/torque of an \doxylink{namespaceiCub}{i\+Cub} leg (left/right) given the FT measurements of the sensor placed in the middle of the leg. 

The sensor parameters are automatically set by chosing left or right during initialization of \doxylink{classiCub_1_1iDyn_1_1iCubLegDyn}{i\+Cub\+Leg\+Dyn}.
